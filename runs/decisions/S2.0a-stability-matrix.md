## Observations

1. **The strategy is now usable across the year.** Pre-rails, this same config produced 100% intra-month equity DD in Mar and a complete account blowout in Apr. With rails, all five months end positive and stay under 30% eq DD.
2. **Feb's +411% is a real outlier**, not a fluke from the data window. It reflects a strongly-ranging month — the grid-martingale's best-case scenario. Worth investigating to understand whether other Feb-like periods are catchable as a leading indicator (Sprint 3 regime work).
3. **Jan's +8% is the weakest month.** Likely a quieter / more directional period where the regime gate blocked many entries. Sprint 3 may want to characterize what made Jan less amenable.
4. **Win rate is remarkably stable (69.6%-73.9%)** across all 5 months despite vastly different P&L. Confirms the grid mechanics work consistently; what changes is depth/duration of losing baskets, which the rails now cap.
5. **Max eq DD (26.6% in May) leaves headroom** under the 40% S1.6 ceiling. Once Sprint 2 produces an adaptive-parameter recipe that further reduces tail risk, we should harden ceiling to 30%, then 25% per the plan.

## Caveats

- Only 5 months sampled, only first half of each month. Need to extend to full months when timeout allows (each full month requires ~3-4 hours of tester time).
- Second-half-of-month windows not yet tested. If second halves systematically differ from first halves (e.g., end-of-month flow effects), the per-month numbers above underestimate variance.
- Only 1 broker-spread profile (`robo`). Axi profile dropped from active queue per user direction; pure-raw-Duka and live RoboForex tick capture deferred to F3 backlog.
- Strategy still vulnerable to regime shifts at intra-month granularity. The rails catch them, but per-trade-cluster economics matter for the live P&L curve. Sprint 3 (better regime classifier, MTF slope) targets this.
